A facility of its kind

Made exclusively for infant nutrition.

Deluxe Foods is Pakistan’s first dedicated plant for infant formula and pediatric diet products—designed around the high standards required for life’s earliest nutrition.

Designed around assurance

Precision has a place.

Our separate purpose-built building houses Pakistan’s largest coupled soft-pack and tin-filling lines. Every part of the facility is planned to protect product integrity, from raw material handling through final packing.

Rounded building edges, coved wall-to-floor joints, and stainless-steel doors and handles reduce areas where unhygienic deposits could collect.

The Deluxe process

Protection at every point.

Advanced manufacturing features work together to support a consistent, controlled production environment.

01

Controlled environment

The 153-foot horizontal production line operates with an HVAC system equipped with HEPA filters, meeting Class I cleanroom specifications.

02

No Human Touch

Complete automation is designed to carry product from raw material handling through finished-product packing with minimal intervention.

03

Air Replacement Technology

During infant formula tin filling, air replacement technology helps prevent oxidation and maintain product quality.

04

Advanced coupled lines

Large-scale soft-pack and tin-filling coupled lines are designed exclusively for infant formula and pediatric diet products.
