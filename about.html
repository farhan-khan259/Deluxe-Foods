Our story

Made for the
first chapter.

Delux Foods is home to Pakistan’s first dedicated plant for infant nutrition. Built on a 95,200 sq. ft. footprint, the facility is designed exclusively for infant formula and pediatric diet products.

Every detail has been considered—from the materials that enter our systems to the packs that leave them. We unite exacting hygiene, intelligent automation and a culture of continuous verification to honour the confidence families place in every product.
